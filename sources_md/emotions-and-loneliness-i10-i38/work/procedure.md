# Procedure

- **Question.** Settled with the evaluator as recorded in `workflow.json` (`question_as_agreed`). It was run as a trial, straight through, with no stops for stakeholders. The coding definitions reuse those of the earlier I01 and I48 emotions run, with one column added: `link` (loneliness, connection, neither).
- **Documents.** Only I10 and I38, named in the `two` sample step. Both were read in full. The rest of `corpus/` is kept unchanged, but none of it was read for this run.
- **First coding.** The analyst coded both interviews against the `emotions` step's prompt and columns. The rows were written by `work/make_rows.py` (output in `work/make_rows.output.txt`) to `coded/emotions.json`. The analyst had already read both interviews for an informal summary earlier in the same conversation, so the first coding was not blind.
- **Recount.** `recount.py` recounted the tables from the rows and checked every quotation against its document (`work/recount_answer.output.txt`). The analyst then wrote `answer.md`, with counts as cell ids and citations as row ids.
- **Second coding.** A fresh Sonnet helper coded both interviews independently under `work/briefs/recode.md`, writing `recode/coded/emotions.json` and `recode/unclear.md`. `recount.py recode` and `compare.py` then wrote `recode/disagreements.md` (`work/recount_recode.output.txt`, `work/compare.output.txt`). Agreement there is at document level, so the check compared the two codings passage by passage.
- **Check.** A fresh Opus helper worked under `work/briefs/check.md`. It ruled on each passage-level disagreement, tightened two definitions in `workflow.json` (loneliness for feelings about being alone; mixed for past and present feelings in one passage) and recoded against them. It added omitted passages, corrected `coded/emotions.json` and `answer.md`, recounted, and wrote `check.md`.
- **Finishing.** The analyst added two figure lines and two section links to `answer.md`, then built `work/evidence-table.md` from the final rows (`work/evidence_table.py`) and recounted.
