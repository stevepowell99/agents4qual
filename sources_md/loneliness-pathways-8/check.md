## Rulings on the disagreements

1. I39, cause Not fitting in or excluded, "this person could feel left out". For the rows. Asked about her drawing of one figure apart from a group, she agrees in her own words ("That's correct, yeah. That is true.") that not being part of the group makes the person lonely, then says "this person could feel left out". Being kept out of a group is Not fitting in or excluded and "left out" is Loneliness; coded others, as she says "Not me personally".
2. I10, cause Other cause, "there are so many other creatives. I think that itself makes everybody feel a bit lonely within their kind of freelancing work". For the second coder. This is a stated link to loneliness: competition among freelancers makes contact impersonal. It fits no listed factor, so Other cause; "everybody" is people in general, so others. Row added.
3. I27, cause Not fitting in or excluded, "it’s difficult to like meet anybody. ‘Cause you feel like they don’t wanna be with somebody that has a baby". For the rows. She expects rejection that has not happened ("you feel like they don’t wanna"), which the definition puts under fear of rejection in Low self-worth and social anxiety; Not fitting in covers being actually judged or rejected.
4. I39, cause Low mood and rumination, "I feel like being upset would sometimes leave you feeling a bit lonely." For the second coder. She states upset leading to loneliness directly, before giving holding it in as a further reason; the first coder coded only the second. She is answering about her own habit of walking when upset, so own; the upset does not come from loneliness, so onset. Row added.
5. I43, cause Unable to open up or be understood, "there is certain stuff you wanna talk about, you're not comfortable with your friends or your family" and "you’re not having there somebody to talk to about, because sometimes friends won’t understand that". For the second coder. Both passages give, with "because", having nobody to confide in or be understood by as the reason he felt lonely before he had a partner and at university. The No partner rows stay, since he names both. Two rows added, own, onset.
6. I43, effect Low self-worth and social anxiety, "The loneliness can trigger that questioning yourself." For the second coder. The questioning he means here is "do I really want to go to work? ... I might as well just stay at home because I have no one", which is lack of motivation, listed under Low mood and rumination, not self-doubt. Effect changed to Low mood and rumination.
7. I08, effect Unable to open up or be understood, "they will find it difficult to kind of socialize and kind of open up if they're in, experiencing loneliness for a long time". Unsettled. The passage names both socialising and opening up, and the definitions did not say where difficulty opening up goes. His next answer is about disclosure ("it's difficult for someone to kind of just openly say a lot of things about themselves"). Unable to open up now counts "finding it hard to open up, confide or disclose things about oneself", and Withdrawing does not count it "even where the passage also names socialising". A search of all eight documents for opening up, confiding and disclosing found no other passage the change moves. Effect changed to Unable to open up or be understood.
8. I10, effect Low mood and rumination, "I just feel it makes me like, feel sad for myself. I feel like I missed out." For the rows. The sadness at losing touch with family is not said to lead to loneliness or withdrawing, so it is not on a chain the prompt codes.
9. I22, effect Low mood and rumination, "it actually did make me really sad. And I feel like- I was like twelve, so I cried for like a year". For the rows. The sadness at losing her dog runs alongside the loneliness she names next; she does not say it leads to loneliness.
10. I39, effect Low mood and rumination, "if it is something that you are constantly just thinking about it to yourself, then that can just have a negative impact overall". For the rows. The effect is "a negative impact overall", not loneliness or withdrawing, and the passage does not carry it on to loneliness.
11. I22, role maintaining, "it doesn’t seem like they care about me so what’s the point in me putting in the effort if they don’t want to". For the second coder. The withdrawing comes from her family not caring, not from loneliness, and the definition puts withdrawing that comes from family conflict under onset. Role changed to onset. I22 then has no maintaining link.

## Rows changed from checking the report

The quoted passage, "as much as you all want to be together, everyone's living a separate life so as well that connection starts to fade away", is about his family's connection fading, not friendships, and states no effect beyond that. The link he states to loneliness follows: "But the loneliness is comes and goes here and there, cause you got nephews and nieces and you see them, sometimes just miss them." The row now quotes that passage, Family conflict or distance to Loneliness, own. Family trouble to loneliness is then direct in all three accounts, and the report's claim that it reaches more accounts through indirect routes is cut.

It is a general "you" ("You don’t want to lose that person ... that’s why when you mourn, you try and isolate yourself"), and read as his own case it would be a hypothetical about his own future, which the prompt does not code. Whose changed to others. Loss to loneliness is then said of the speaker's own life by I22 only.
